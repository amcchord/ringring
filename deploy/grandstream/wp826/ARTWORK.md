# WP826 wallpaper artwork

The three masters were created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool in new-image mode, then center-cropped and reduced to 240×320 by `tools/prepare_wallpapers.py`. The generated masters are intentionally not required at runtime.

## Day prompt

> Create a portrait 3:4 abstract Memphis-design wallpaper master for the RingRing family VoIP handset, DAY variant. Warm cream paper background (#FFFAF1) with a sparse, joyful arrangement of chunky geometric shapes: deep purple (#6C4CFF), coral red (#F7505A), sunny yellow (#FFD84D), mint (#8EE7CF), bubblegum pink (#FFB6D5), sky blue (#86C9FF), all outlined selectively in dark aubergine ink (#201A3A). Include playful arcs suggesting ringing sound waves, dots, squiggles, half-circles, and rounded blocks, but no literal telephone, no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no gradients, no shadows, no tiny details, and no watermark. Keep the central 45% calm and uncluttered for a phone UI overlay; distribute larger motifs around the outer edges. Crisp flat vector-like raster artwork, strong contrast, child-friendly but sophisticated, suitable for reduction to exactly 240×320 pixels.

## Twilight prompt

> Create a portrait 3:4 abstract Memphis-design wallpaper master for the RingRing family VoIP handset, TWILIGHT PURPLE variant. Solid deep purple background (#6C4CFF) with sparse flat geometric motifs placed mainly at the outer edges: warm cream (#FFFAF1), coral (#F7505A), yellow (#FFD84D), mint (#8EE7CF), pink (#FFB6D5), and sky blue (#86C9FF), with selective dark aubergine outlines (#201A3A). Use playful ringing-wave arcs, dots, short squiggles, half-circles, and rounded blocks. No telephone object, no people, no words, no letters, no numbers, no brand mark, no gradients, no lighting, no shadows, no texture, no tiny details, and no watermark. Preserve a calm, mostly empty central 45% for legible phone UI text. Crisp screen-printed flat vector-like raster artwork; fun Memphis style, high contrast, suitable for reduction to exactly 240×320 pixels.

## Party prompt

> Create a portrait 3:4 abstract Memphis-design wallpaper master for the RingRing family VoIP handset, PARTY YELLOW variant. Solid sunny yellow background (#FFD84D) with a sparse festive border of large, simple Memphis shapes in deep purple (#6C4CFF), coral (#F7505A), mint (#8EE7CF), bubblegum pink (#FFB6D5), sky blue (#86C9FF), warm cream (#FFFAF1), and dark aubergine (#201A3A). Motifs may include ringing sound-wave arcs, confetti dots, short squiggles, half-circles, arches, and rounded blocks. No literal phone, no people, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no gradients, no lighting, no shadows, no texture, no tiny details, and no watermark. Keep the central 45% mostly empty and quiet for a phone's account and status overlay; concentrate motifs at the outer edges. Crisp flat vector-like raster design, bold joyful Memphis look, suitable for reduction to exactly 240×320 pixels.
